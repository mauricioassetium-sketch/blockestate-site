**Thrown away on purpose, and why it matters:** the search returned `Cycling next to the Danube
(Esztergom, Hungary)` for **Danube Properties**, `Azizi Mosque` for **Azizi Developments** and `Duke
Ellington house` for **Ellington Properties**. Three photographs, three developers, three names that
merely look alike — a mosque, a Hungarian river and a jazz musician's house. Having permission to use
a picture is not the same as the picture being of the right thing: **a photograph of the Danube river
is not a photograph of Danube Properties**, and it would have gone on the site looking perfectly
legal. Danube, Azizi and Ellington are still without a project photograph; Sobha and Binghatti too.
