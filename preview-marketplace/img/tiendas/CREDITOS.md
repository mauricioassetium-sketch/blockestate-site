# Store badges

The official App Store and Google Play badges, taken from Wikimedia Commons. They are the marks of
their owners and are meant to be shown only next to a real download link.

| Badge | File | Source |
|---|---|---|
| appgallery | `img/tiendas/appgallery.png` | Wikimedia Commons, `Huawei AppGallery white badge TR.png` |
| appstore | `img/tiendas/appstore.png` | Wikimedia Commons, `Download on the App Store Badge FRCA RGB blk.svg` |
| googleplay | `img/tiendas/googleplay.png` | Wikimedia Commons, `Google Play Store badge EN.svg` |

**AppGallery is not here as a badge**: the only file in a free repository carries Turkish wording, and
a Turkish button on an English site is worse than a plain one. It stays as text until there is an
official English badge.

**And the honest line that goes with them:** native apps are **not part of Release 1** — they are
outside the baseline of the BRD. The badges are on the page for when the apps exist; until then they
are pictures, not links.
